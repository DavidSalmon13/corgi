## Summary

Both roles see a policy's full history and financial statement, and can open "how we got this" on any amount to trace it to the ledger entry, the policy change or payment behind it, and the Stripe event that settled it. The trial scenario (an add-on's start moved from Oct 15 to Oct 8, +$14.00) reads clearly, with the original charge, the reversal and the rebill all visible. The policyholder also gets Resend emails when a policy is issued and when a payment or refund settles, which makes Resend the trial's second live sandbox integration.
